01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

Modernizing a complex document lifecycle.

Our capstone explores how workflow automation can reduce repetitive intake tasks while preserving business-rule checks and human oversight.

THE CHALLENGE↗

Manual processing creates friction.

Special Claims and Voucher packets arrive with supporting documents that must be identified, organized, and checked. Manual handling can increase repetitive effort, inconsistency, and the time needed to resolve incomplete submissions.

PROJECT CONTEXT · NATIONAL HOUSING COMPLIANCE

OUR APPROACH⚡

Automate the repeatable.

Use Power Automate to coordinate intake, document movement, routing, and notifications, with SharePoint as the organized document repository.

OUR SAFEGUARD◎

Keep people in control.

Apply deterministic validation and independently verify AI-assisted suggestions. Missing, ambiguous, or unverified information is directed to manual review.

BUSINESS PROCESS & DESIGN

How the proposed system works

CURRENT PROCESS

Challenges identified

  • Claims and Voucher submissions arrive at designated email accounts and depend on subject information for identification.
  • Malformed subjects can interrupt processing and require manual correction.
  • Existing SharePoint/custom logic does not directly transfer to SharePoint Online.
  • Failed validation, routing and corrections create repetitive employee work.
FUTURE-STATE PROCESS

Automation with safeguards

Email intake → capture → identify submission → validate → AI assistance when needed → independent verification → SharePoint storage → routing.

Missing data, low confidence, duplicates, failed checks and workflow errors go to human review. Deterministic business rules take priority over AI interpretation.

DOCUMENT PATHS

Special Claims and Voucher Packets

SPECIAL CLAIMS

Claim-specific workflow

Validate PDF, contract number, claim type and required information. Identify Georgia or Illinois and classify Regular Vacancy versus Unpaid Rent/Damages. Look for an existing Claim ID; add to an existing claim or create a new claim record and SharePoint folder. Failed checks go to manual review.

VOUCHER PACKETS

Voucher-specific workflow

Validate PDF, contract number, reporting month/year and required information. Identify state, rename the file using the proposed contract/date naming convention, populate voucher metadata and store it in the appropriate SharePoint area. Invalid submissions go to manual review.

LESSONS LEARNED

Design principles

  • Verify AI results before continuing automated processing.
  • Retain human review for uncertain or unverified cases.
  • Separate configurable business settings from core workflow logic.
  • Test expected decision branches, not just successful flow execution.
  • Coordinate Power Automate, SharePoint and AI dependencies early.
